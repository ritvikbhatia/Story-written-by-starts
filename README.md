# 9 Months Webpage

A simple one-page website to celebrate 9 months together.

## Customize
- Replace placeholder photos in `assets/photos/`:
  - `photo1.jpg`
  - `photo2.jpg`
  - `photo3.jpg`
- Replace `assets/audio/message.mp3` with your voice note.
- Edit text in `index.html` to match your story and exact commitments.

## Run locally
Open `index.html` in your browser.

## Files
- `index.html` - page structure and content
- `style.css` - styling
- `script.js` - small interactions for reveal cards
- `assets/` - photos and audio
